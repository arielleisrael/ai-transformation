# **Fit-Adjusted Economic Opportunity (FAEO) Scorecard**

**Purpose:** Rank opportunities by commercial attractiveness and founder fit so we pursue the best economic opportunity—not the most exciting idea.

**Scale:** Score each criterion from 1–5. Use evidence from the qualification form, Diagnostic, company research, and technical review. Unknowns should not be treated as 5s; use the lowest score defensible from the evidence and flag the unknown.

| Criterion | What It Measures | 1 — Weak | 3 — Moderate | 5 — Strong |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Pain | How costly/disruptive is the problem? | Minor inconvenience; little measurable impact | Material cost, delay, rework, or capacity drain | Severe/strategic pain with obvious economic consequences |
| Urgency | How strongly must this be solved now? | Nice-to-have; no trigger/timeline | Important this quarter/near term | Active executive priority, deadline, or escalating pain |
| Budget | Is there a credible ability/willingness to pay? | No budget signal / price resistance likely | Plausible budget; needs approval | Budget owner engaged or clear spend authority/funding |
| Access | How close are we to a buying decision? | No access to owner/decision-maker | Strong influencer can bring decision-maker | Direct access to decision-maker/budget owner |
| AI Readiness | Will we spend time solving the workflow rather than selling AI itself? | Resistant/no adoption | Active experimentation / some tooling | Leadership-sponsored AI use with tools/data access |
| Uzziah \+ Arielle Fit | How directly do our proven strengths map to the problem? | Major skill gap / outside credible scope | Good fit with some learning/partner needs | Excellent fit across workflow, transformation, engineering, automation, QA |
| Delivery Simplicity | Can we deliver a meaningful result without excessive complexity? | Large dependencies/long build/high uncertainty | Manageable with defined dependencies | Tightly scoped, simple architecture, fast implementation |
| Repeatability | Could similar buyers have the same problem? | Highly bespoke one-off | Some reusable pattern | Common/repeatable workflow with reusable assets |
| Proof Speed | How quickly can we demonstrate value? | Months before credible result | 4–8 weeks | Days to a few weeks with measurable before/after |

## **Interpretation**

* Maximum score: 45\.  
* 37–45 \= Priority opportunity. Strong candidate for immediate commercial pursuit if no red-flag technical or ethical issue exists.  
* 29–36 \= Promising. Pursue if key unknowns can be resolved quickly or if higher-scoring opportunities are absent.  
* 21–28 \= Weak / nurture. Do not let it displace stronger opportunities; identify exactly what would need to improve.  
* 9–20 \= Reject / not now. Poor fit for the current sprint.

**Tie-breaker rule:** If two opportunities score similarly, prefer the one with the shortest path to a paid decision and measurable proof, provided the economics remain meaningful.

**Non-negotiable override:** A high numeric score does not override a material technical, legal, security, ethics, or trust concern. Such concerns must be resolved before proposal.
