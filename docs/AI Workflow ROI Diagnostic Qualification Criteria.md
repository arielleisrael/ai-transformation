# **AI Workflow ROI Diagnostic Qualification Criteria**

**Objective:** Protect founder time. A prospect receives a Diagnostic only when there is enough company fit, problem signal, AI readiness, decision access, and potential economic value to justify a live conversation.

## **Required Minimum Criteria**

**Company fit:** Generally 50–250 employees and knowledge-work-heavy. Exceptions require a clear reason the prospect still has attractive economics and a short enough buying cycle.

**AI interest/readiness:** Already using, piloting, budgeting for, or actively exploring AI/automation. We do not want to spend the sales cycle convincing a resistant company that AI matters.

**Meaningful workflow:** They can name at least one real workflow/process that is repetitive, manual, slow, error-prone, coordination-heavy, or expensive.

**Potential economic value:** The problem plausibly affects enough people, hours, throughput, quality, revenue, or risk that a paid implementation could create material value.

**Decision access:** The respondent is a decision-maker, budget owner, workflow owner, or credible influencer who can bring the right people into the process.

**Enough information:** The qualification form provides sufficient detail to enter the call with a real hypothesis rather than a vague “tell us about AI” conversation.

## **Automatic Reject / Do Not Schedule Yet**

* Primarily wants general AI education, brainstorming, or free consulting without a specific workflow problem.  
* No identifiable operational pain or meaningful cost; the workflow is too trivial to support an economically rational engagement.  
* Company is fundamentally resistant to AI and the conversation would first require us to sell the category itself.  
* Respondent has no meaningful access to the workflow owner or buying process and cannot make an introduction.  
* The stated need is clearly outside our current capability/scope (for example, deep regulated infrastructure/security work requiring specialized credentials or a large engineering team).  
* Obvious expectation that we build or design the solution for free before any commercial commitment.

## **Qualification Decision**

| Decision | When to Use | Next Action |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| ACCEPT | All core criteria are met or strongly indicated. | Schedule the Diagnostic. |
| HOLD / NEED MORE INFO | Potential fit, but one material qualification item is unknown. | Ask 1–3 targeted follow-up questions before scheduling. |
| REJECT / NOT NOW | One or more core criteria clearly fail and no reasonable exception exists. | Thank them, record the reason, and do not consume founder call time. |
