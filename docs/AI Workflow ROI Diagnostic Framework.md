# **AI Workflow ROI Diagnostic Framework**

**Purpose:** Identify one expensive workflow inside a qualified company, quantify the business cost of the current process, determine whether AI/automation can materially improve it, and decide whether a paid implementation engagement is justified.

**Simple promise:** Show us one expensive, manual, or inefficient workflow. We will help determine where AI/automation could materially improve it, what the opportunity may be worth, and whether it is sensible to implement.

## **Who It Is For**

* Organizations that generally fit our initial ICP: 50–250 employees, knowledge-work-heavy, already using or actively experimenting with AI, but without mature end-to-end ownership of AI workflow transformation.  
* Business leaders or strong internal influencers who can identify a meaningful workflow problem and help move a worthwhile opportunity toward a buying decision.  
* Companies with a repetitive, manual, slow, error-prone, or coordination-heavy workflow that consumes meaningful employee time or constrains business performance.

## **Format**

* Qualification form completed before scheduling.  
* 30-minute live Diagnostic by video call; extend to 45 minutes only when the workflow legitimately requires deeper technical discovery.  
* Uzziah leads business/workflow discovery and economics. Arielle joins or supports when technical feasibility, systems, integrations, data, security, or implementation complexity materially affect the opportunity.  
* No implementation work is performed during the Diagnostic.

## **What We Gather**

* Business context: company, team, workflow owner, buyer/influencer, why this matters now.  
* Current workflow: trigger, major steps, people involved, tools/systems, handoffs, frequency, volume, time spent, delays, errors/rework, and current use of AI.  
* Economic picture: approximate labor/time cost, business consequences, opportunity cost, and what improvement would be meaningful.  
* AI readiness: tools already in use, internal owner, data/system access, known technical or security constraints.  
* Commercial context: urgency, decision process, authority/influence, budget signal, timeline, and what would need to be true for a paid implementation to move forward.

## **What the Prospect Receives**

* A concise verbal/readout summary of the workflow problem we heard.  
* An initial view of whether the workflow appears to be a strong, moderate, or weak candidate for AI/automation.  
* A preliminary estimate or range of the economic opportunity when enough information is available; assumptions are clearly labeled.  
* A recommended next step: no action, gather missing information, pursue an internal improvement, or consider a paid implementation engagement with us.

## **Boundary: What Is NOT Included**

* No free custom automation, coding, prompt library, system build, production architecture, integrations, or implementation plan detailed enough to execute without us.  
* No security/compliance certification, production-readiness guarantee, or deep technical audit during the introductory Diagnostic.  
* No promise of savings or ROI before the relevant assumptions and baseline have been validated.  
* If the Diagnostic reveals a qualified opportunity, detailed solution design belongs inside the paid engagement/proposal process.
